replace permuteNetIds O(N!) recursion with sound bitmask DP

The recursive permutation enumeration in permuteNetIds() can walk every
one of N! topo-NIC orderings before returning. On systems with 16 NICs
and NCCL_IB_MERGE_NICS=0 the no-match path takes ~1.7 * 16! ~= 35
trillion function calls, on the order of days of CPU walk inside a
single ncclCommInitRank() call. RCCL effectively hangs at init.

Replace the recursion with a memoized bitmask DP. Crucially, the NUMA
and gdrLevel predicates are checked inside the per-step transition,
not at the leaf. This is what makes dp[mask] a sound memoization key:
once a (pos, i) pairing has been accepted, feasibility from (mask,
pos+1) onward depends only on which topo NICs remain. The naive
formulation that checks gdrLevel only at the leaf is unsound on inputs
with duplicate nicNuma entries (every shipped rcclRomeModel has them),
because the first failing leaf poisons memo[full_mask] and every valid
sibling permutation reaches the cached false and is missed.

Also preserves the PATH_PXB <-> PATH_PIX equivalence rule that was
added to the leaf check at rome_models.cc:2431-2433 after the
ROCm/rccl -> ROCm/rocm-systems migration, and adds an N > 30 guard
plus 1u<<N to keep the dp size computation in unsigned arithmetic.

Re-applies the optimization originally proposed in ROCm/rccl#1566
(closed for procedural reasons during the migration), with a fix to
the DP formulation.
